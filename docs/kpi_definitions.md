# FieldSignal AI — KPI and Metric Definitions

---

## Dashboard KPIs

These metrics are computed by `lib/production.ts → computeKPIs()` from the year-filtered EIA dataset and displayed in the **KPI Band** at the top of the dashboard.

| KPI | What It Means | Formula / Calculation | Data Source | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Active States** | Number of states with at least one non-zero oil production record in the selected year range | `count(distinct region where oil value > 0)` | EIA historical | Indicates data coverage. Fewer than 8 active states suggests a coverage gap or narrow filter range. |
| **Top Oil State** | The U.S. state with the highest crude oil output in the most recent year of the selected range | `argmax(oil value by region, year = latestYear)` | EIA historical | Identifies concentration — a single state dominating output is a portfolio risk signal. |
| **Top Oil State Share %** | Top state's production as a percentage of the national total in the latest year | `topStateOil / totalOilLatestYear × 100` | EIA historical | Concentration risk metric. Share above 50% triggers an Action Center alert. |
| **Total Oil (Latest Year)** | Aggregate crude oil production across all 10 tracked states for the most recent year in the range | `sum(oil value) where year = latestYear` | EIA historical | The headline production figure. Unit: thousand barrels (Kbbl). |
| **YoY Oil Growth %** | Year-over-year percentage change in total oil production | `(totalOil[latestYear] − totalOil[latestYear − 1]) / totalOil[latestYear − 1] × 100` | EIA historical | Trend direction. Negative growth below −5% triggers a High severity alert. |
| **Projected 2028 Oil** | Estimated total crude oil production in 2028 based on the CAGR forecast model | See Forecast Model below | FieldSignal model | Forward-looking headline figure for planning horizon. Labeled "FORECAST" — not an EIA projection. |

## Forecast Model Outputs

Computed by `lib/forecast.ts → extendWithForecast()`.

| Metric | What It Means | Calculation | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Forecast CAGR** | The compound annual growth rate applied to extend the historical series | Fitted to the last 5 non-zero annual data points using `ln(end/start) / years`; clamped to [−5%, +20%] | FieldSignal model | Clamping prevents implausible extrapolation from volatile short series |
| **Forecast Quality Score** | A 0–1 composite score expressing confidence in the CAGR projection | `(yearsScore + stabilityScore) / 2` where `yearsScore = min(1, n / 10)` and `stabilityScore = max(0, 1 − σ(yoyRates) / 0.30)` | FieldSignal model | Surfaced in the Confidence Strip and as a label (High ≥ 0.70, Medium 0.40–0.69, Low < 0.40) |
| **Forecast Quality Label** | Human-readable tier derived from the quality score | High ≥ 0.70 · Medium 0.40–0.69 · Low < 0.40 | FieldSignal model | "Low" quality triggers an Action Center heuristic alert |

## Action Center Heuristics

Generated by `lib/insights.ts → generateInsights()`. Six independent checks evaluated on every render; sorted by severity; padded to a minimum of 3 entries with monitoring rows.

| Heuristic | Severity | Trigger Condition | What It Signals |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Concentration Risk** | High (share > 65%) or Medium (share 50–65%) | Top state share > 50% of total production | Over-reliance on a single region; supply disruption risk |
| **Production Decline** | High | National YoY oil growth < −5% | Broad-based output contraction; investment thesis at risk |
| **Coverage Warning** | Medium | Fewer than 8 states reporting data | Incomplete picture; filter range may be too narrow |
| **Forecast Fragility** | Medium | Forecast quality score < 0.50 | Sparse or volatile history reduces projection reliability |
| **State Outperformance** | Opportunity | Focused state YoY growth > national average + 5 pp | Relative alpha; focused state growing materially faster |
| **Strong National Growth** | Opportunity | National YoY oil growth > 8% | Broad-based production expansion |

## Scenario Simulator Outputs

Computed by `lib/scenario.ts → runScenario()`.

| Output | What It Means | Calculation | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Projected 2028 (Scenario)** | 2028 oil production estimate under the selected scenario | `lastHistoricalValue × (1 + cagrUsed)^yearsToHorizon` | FieldSignal scenario model |
| **Delta vs Base Case** | Absolute difference from the Base scenario 2028 projection | `scenarioProjected2028 − baseProjected2028` | FieldSignal scenario model |
| **Delta vs Base Case %** | Percentage difference from the Base scenario | `delta / baseProjected2028 × 100` | FieldSignal scenario model |
| **Applied CAGR** | The growth rate actually used in the projection | Base CAGR (Downside −2%, Base +2.5%, Upside +5.5%) adjusted by the slider value (±5 pp), clamped to [−10%, +25%] | FieldSignal scenario model |
| **Confidence Label** | Scenario reliability tier | High: Base + slider < 100 bps; Low: Upside or CAGR > 7%; Medium: otherwise | FieldSignal model |
| **Top Risk** | Contextual narrative of the primary downside risk | Deterministic rule-based text based on scenario type and CAGR magnitude | FieldSignal model |
| **Diversification Candidate** | State recommended for geographic diversification | Rule-based selection: lowest-risk growing state given the top oil state and scenario context | FieldSignal model |

## Analytical Metrics — Well Economics Calculator

Computed by `lib/wellEconomics.ts → computeWellEconomics()`. All outputs are illustrative model estimates for a single horizontal oil well under exponential decline. Not investment advice.

| Metric | What It Means | Formula / Calculation | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **EUR (Kbbl)** | Estimated ultimate recovery — total oil produced over the well's life | `Σ q(t) × DAYS_PER_MONTH` over all months; `q(t) = q_i × e^(−D × t)`, midpoint-of-month convention | Undiscounted; oil only |
| **NPV @ 10%** | Net present value of the well's cash flows discounted at the user-specified rate (default 10%) | `−capex + Σ NCF_m / (1 + r)^(m/12)` where `NCF_m = revenue_m − monthly_LOE` | Positive NPV = project returns more than the hurdle rate |
| **IRR (%)** | The discount rate at which NPV = 0 | 120-iteration bisection over monthly cash flows; range [−99.99%, +900%]; only positive IRR surfaced | Null if the project never achieves payback; displayed as "< 0%" in that case |
| **Payback (months)** | Number of months until cumulative undiscounted cash flows recover the initial capex | First month `m` where `−capex + Σ NCF_1..m ≥ 0` | Displayed as `> N mo` if break-even is not reached within the well life |
| **Breakeven Price ($/bbl)** | The oil price at which NPV = 0 | Closed-form: `price = (capex + LOE × Σdf_m) / Σ(prod_m × df_m)` | Avoids a second numerical solve; one pass during cash flow loop |

### Inputs and Defaults

| Input | Default | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Initial Rate | 300 bbl/day | 10–2,000 | Auto-loaded from state preset when a state is focused |
| Annual Decline | 35%/yr | 1–80% | Auto-loaded from state preset |
| Well Life | 10 years | 1–30 | User-controlled; not overwritten by state preset |
| Oil Price | $70/bbl | $10–$200 | User-controlled; not overwritten by state preset |

| Category | Backed by | Label in UI |
|---|---|---|
| Historical KPI numbers | EIA Open Data API v2 | "EIA data" / "Based on EIA data" |
| Forecast projection | FieldSignal CAGR model | "FORECAST" badge |
| Scenario projections | FieldSignal scenario model | "Scenario estimate" / "FieldSignal model estimates" |
| Action Center alerts | Deterministic heuristics on EIA data | "6 heuristics evaluated" |
| Well economics outputs | Illustrative decline curve model | "Illustrative model" badge |
| AI analyst responses | Claude API + AnalystContext | Streamed from Claude; context is EIA-derived |
